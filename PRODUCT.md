# Call Intelligence

## What it is
Listens to every inbound phone call at a home-services company (pest control), transcribes it, classifies it (new sale, cancellation, re-service, scheduling, billing, general), scores it against the company's four-part call framework (Validation, Understand, Solve, Verify), and turns misses into coaching and follow-up tasks.

## Who uses it
- **Owner / office manager**, primary. Opens it at a desk between jobs, morning and end of day, on a laptop; sometimes a phone in the truck. Wants to know in seconds what is costing money, which rep needs coaching, and what to do next.
- **Customer service reps**, secondary. Open one call to hear the moment they missed and the words to say instead.

## Jobs
1. See money at risk: unbooked leads, lost cancels, unhappy customers.
2. Find the one step the team misses most and coach it.
3. Open a call: listen, read the transcript, see exactly why each step passed or failed.
4. Assign follow-ups and mark them done.
5. Upload a recording and get it scored.

## Truths to keep
- Scores are evidence-backed: every passed step quotes the rep's own words.
- Score bands: Gold, Green, Below (Below splits into close to green, needs work, critical).
- Dataset: 16 calls, 5 reps, company Northwind Pest Co.
